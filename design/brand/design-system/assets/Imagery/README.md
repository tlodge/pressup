# Imagery

Photography is black and white, high contrast and shot in real gyms, of real members mid-effort. Yellow is the only colour that ever touches it, as type or rings.

- `take-the-first-step.jpg`, `discipline.jpg`, `locked-in.jpg`, `focus.jpg`: the reference posters. Copy their composition, not their files.
- `photo-shoulder.jpg` (3:4), `photo-row.jpg` (1:2), `photo-row-wide.jpg` (16:9): text-free crops of the poster photography for cards and hero placeholders. Swap them for proper shoots when available.

Use: desaturate fully, push contrast, and add motion blur to show effort. Avoid stock smiles, colour photos, posed mirror selfies, and brand logos on kit.
