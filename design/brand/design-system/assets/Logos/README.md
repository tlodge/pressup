# Logos

The PressUp logo is one round-capped line: the lower bowl of a P in white, and a yellow arc with a ring on its end, a figure mid press-up. These are transparent PNGs cut from the 300 dpi master.

- `pressup-wordmark-on-dark.png`: yellow arc and white letters. The default, on `surface` (dark), black photography and the web.
- `pressup-wordmark-on-light.png`: yellow arc and ink letters, on light `surface` at 32px tall or more. Smaller than that, the yellow arc is too faint on white, so use mono-black.
- `pressup-wordmark-mono-white.png`: all white, over busy photography.
- `pressup-wordmark-mono-black.png`: all ink, on a `press-yellow` fill, on small light placements, and for one-colour print.
- `pressup-mark-*.png`: the P mark alone, in the same four inks. App icon, favicon, avatar and social profile. Min size 24px.

Clear space: the height of the mark's ring on all sides. Never recolour the arc, outline, stretch, add effects, or set the wordmark in a font.
