# ProgrammeCard

A guided programme on a black-and-white photograph, with its name as a `[ BRACKET ]` title. This is the poster language at card size.

The photo is forced to greyscale with a `scrim` gradient at the base, so `press-yellow` and white type always read. The rings sit off-centre over the image. The card is always dark, whatever the theme: it is a piece of the poster, not a surface.

**Consumer provides:** `title` (one or two words), `image` (URL of a photo of a real person training, ideally 4:5), `imageAlt`, optional `weeks`, `perWeek`, `level`, `description` (one sentence), `action` (e.g. a `Button`), `rings` (false to hide).
